The problem

Your pipeline problem isn't effort. It's the model.

Every way the market sells you top-of-funnel has the same flaw: the cost recurs, and the capability never becomes yours.

The SDR treadmill

03

04

Data that dies

FAQ

The questions every buyer asks.

What exactly gets installed?
A complete top-of-funnel pipeline in your own stack: ICP and target-account definition, a sourcing and enrichment pipeline (Clay, Apollo), sequenced outbound in your brand voice, a deterministic reply-classification gate, CRM dedup and automatic logging, orchestration in n8n, and full documentation. Nothing proprietary — nothing you couldn't hire for tomorrow.
How is this different from an agency retainer?
A retainer is structured so the value lives with the agency — their tooling, their logins, a minimum term, a playbook you never see. This is a fixed-scope build inside your stack. When it ships, it's yours, and it keeps running whether or not we ever work together again. Maintenance exists, but it's opt-in and flat — never a toll.
How is this different from an AI SDR tool?
AI-SDR products are seats you rent, and most are autonomous agents that improvise. This system is deterministic: every sequence and every rule is reviewed by you before anything sends, with hallucination and false-positive guardrails at the reply gate. And when you stop paying a tool, it vanishes. This doesn't — you own it.
Do you guarantee meetings?
No — and be suspicious of anyone who does, because they don't control your offer or your market. What I guarantee is what I do control: the diagnostic produces three actionable fixes or it's free, and the system runs as scoped or I keep building at no charge until it does. That's more than any retainer will put in writing.
What does the build cost?
The diagnostic is a flat $3,500, and every dollar credits toward the build. The build itself is a fixed price quoted in your proposal — scoped to your stack, not a menu. As an anchor: most builds land at a fraction of one SDR-year, and it's a one-time cost instead of a recurring one.
What happens after handoff? What if it breaks?
You get full documentation and a working handoff — your team can run it, or any competent operator can, because it's built on standard tools. If you'd rather not think about it, the optional care plan covers monitoring, deliverability, and iteration for a flat monthly fee you can cancel anytime.
Who actually does the work?
Me. One engineer, zero account managers, senior all the way down. I run this exact outbound motion every day for my own pipeline — 150+ production systems shipped. The person on the sales call, the diagnostic, and the build is the same person.
