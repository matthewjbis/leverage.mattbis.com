The status quo

The math on one SDR.

A rep you hire this week isn't selling until Q2. And won't be here in two years.

The real cost of an SDR isn't the base salary. Add benefits, tooling and data seats, a three-month ramp where they generate nothing, and a manager's time to coach them — and one rep lands between $100K and $170K a year, fully loaded.

Median tenure is under two years and turnover runs 34–40%. So you're not hiring once. You're re-hiring and re-ramping on a loop, and a five-person desk becomes a half-million-dollar line item that resets every couple of years.

A system doesn't ramp. It doesn't churn, and it never needs backfilling. It runs the same top-of-funnel work every day, for a one-time build, and it's yours.
